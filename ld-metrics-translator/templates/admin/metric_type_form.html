About Metric Types

Metric Types classify the measurement approach or methodology used to capture learning and development data.

Common Metric Types:
  • Operational KPI: Key performance indicators for L&D operations
  • Behavioral Metric: Measures changes in learner behavior
  • Business Impact: Direct business outcomes from L&D
  • Learning Analytics: Data-driven insights from learning platforms
  • ROI Metric: Return on investment calculations
Choose types that reflect how the metric is measured or calculated.
