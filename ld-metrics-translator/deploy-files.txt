Essential files to upload to Google Cloud Shell:

REQUIRED FILES:
✅ app.yaml
✅ main.py
✅ requirements.txt (or requirements-prod.txt)
✅ .gcloudignore
✅ config.py
✅ run.py (backup entry point)

REQUIRED DIRECTORIES:
✅ app/ (entire directory)
✅ static/ (entire directory) 
✅ templates/ (entire directory)

OPTIONAL FILES (for production):
- .env.example
- README.md
- Dockerfile (not needed for App Engine)

FILES TO EXCLUDE (don't upload):
❌ venv/
❌ __pycache__/
❌ .git/
❌ logs/
❌ ld_metrics.db (will be recreated)
❌ instance/
❌ tests/
